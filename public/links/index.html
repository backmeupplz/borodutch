Borodutch Labs

Open-source apps, developer tools and games by Nikita Kolmogorov. Every project from our videos is linked below.

For developers

  • TugportDeploy your apps to servers you own with one Rust binary, an open-source alternative to Heroku and Vercel.
  • agentboardA small realtime kanban board that your AI agents and you work on together.
  • plainOne small binary that compiles, tests and deploys Solidity contracts in seconds instead of minutes.
  • MyGroundA self-hosting platform for running useful services on your own home server.

Apps

  • QuilcastA Farcaster client on Hypersnap that runs straight from your browser.
  • Mesh+Message your friends over Meshtastic radios when there is no phone signal.
  • ABS+A small, fast Audiobookshelf player for Android and iPhone.
  • TodorantA todo manager built on 17 rules that help you actually finish your todos.
  • Plain WalletA very minimal EVM wallet whose whole code you can read in an evening.
  • VoicyA Telegram bot that turns voice messages into text in your chats.
  • VideoDownloadBotA Telegram bot that downloads videos from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X by link.
  • BanofbotA Telegram bot that lets group members vote a spammer or troll out.
  • RandyA Telegram bot that runs raffles and giveaways in groups and channels.
  • Jev Anti-SpamA Telegram bot that deletes spam in groups and leaves real messages alone.
