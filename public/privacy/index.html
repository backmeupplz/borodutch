Privacy policy

Last updated on October 28, 2026.

Borodutch Labs is Nikita Kolmogorov's studio. Its publishing tools, including the Meta app "Borodutch Labs Publisher" and its Threads app, do one thing: they post videos we made to the Borodutch Labs accounts on Instagram, Facebook and Threads, which we own.

The tools don't log anyone in except us, and they don't read, store or share any data about other people. The only data they handle is our own: the videos, their captions, and the links and view counts of our own posts.

Access tokens for our accounts are kept in an encrypted password vault and are used only to publish to those accounts. You can ask us to delete anything we hold, or ask any question about this policy, by writing to nikita@borodutch.com.

The Borodutch Labs projects have privacy policies of their own on their sites, all listed on borodutch.com/links.
